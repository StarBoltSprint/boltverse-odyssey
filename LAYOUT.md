## Frozen (never change, any paint)

- **Depth** of the hall (how far the doors sit in Z)
- **Door-to-door spacing** (cyan L ↔ gold R). Same gap every style.
- **Bolt-to-door distance** (spawn small at center; at-A / at-B standing at that sill, same taille bands)
- Camera **lock-off**, 9:16, 720×1280
- Two doors: energy **cyan-teal LEFT**, **gold-orange RIGHT**
- Spawn: **two luminous paths on the floor** (Y-fork from paws → both sills)
- Bolt: full-white GSD, BACK, standing, teal collar

## May change (this is the player's room)

- Hall **décor** / materials (ice, moss, ember, dusk, …) from `catalog/<slot>.md` + player stills
- **Door shape** (oval or RECT fill) — not wood, not chrome UI
- Skin **on top** of the white coat (ember glow, rime, …) — not a different dog

## Restyle path

One **SEALED** ice still → **[Imagine Agent](https://grok.com/imagine/agent)** restyles hall only.

> Same camera. Same depth. Same door spacing. Same Bolt-to-door distance. Same Y-fork. Same pose / taille / place. Completely new décor OK. Door *shape* may adapt. Never move the doors closer/farther. Never grow/shrink the hall. Never invent a new dog.

`imagineStill` / Chat `imagine_image_to_image` / `imagine_reference_to_image` = **BANNED for restyle.** They drift depth and spacing. Grok Build chat cannot pin layout the way Agent can. Instruct the human (or yourself) to open Imagine Agent with the frost still as the plate.
