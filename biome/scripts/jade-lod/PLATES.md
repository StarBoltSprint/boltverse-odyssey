# Plate tableau prompts

Law [48](../../docs/48-jade-plate-cook.md). One biome per cook. Ember and Jade share the kitchen. The palette is the only change. No binaries in this folder. No posters invented here.

Hang this suffix on every ground cook and every sky cook:

```
loopable, first frame matches last frame, no camera move, no dog, no wolf, no German Shepherd, no creature, no text, no UI
```

Copy the light lock verbatim onto both prompts of that biome. Sheets use the same phrase.

## Ember

Light lock:

```
blood-red moon locked upper-center, volcanic dusk, lava key from the cracks, cool ash fill, no second sun, no daylight
```

Ground. 2048² tile. Cracked basalt, luminous cracks that wander, lava sheen, ash grain. The crack is the film's feel. No paved road. No straight south highway.

Sky. 2048×1024 lat-long, or a 2048² dome. One moon locked to the same azimuth for the whole loop. Red vault. Gothic spires as silhouettes, not land. Horizon bottom 10% is ash-red, cousin to the ground's far edge.

Then the suffix. No blood-moon drift. No daylight. No Jade moss.

## Jade

Light lock:

```
late-day gold key upper-left, cool cyan canopy fill, no second sun, no blood moon, no noon
```

Ground. 2048² tile. Wet moss, fine roots, décor pebbles, no paving. No readable near trunks.

Sky. Canopy-filtered mist. Two or three distant blobs. Distant woods as haze only, no countable trunks. Horizon bottom 10% is drowned gold-green. A teal lid on gold ground is a recook.

Then the suffix. No blood moon. No noon.

## Both

Loop 8–10 s. First frame matches the last. Muted. Zero camera. The poster is frame 0 and waits until the mp4 decodes. Far may sit in the plate. Near must not. Forbidden: wolf, dog, Bolt, UI, text, paved path, near trunk, crystal, ruin, second sun, truck, yaw in the clip.
