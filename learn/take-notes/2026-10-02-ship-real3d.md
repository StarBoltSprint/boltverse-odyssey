## Verdict

Use this path for a hero object when the invisible mesh sits in the Imagine silhouettes. On this ship it is not better than showing the stills. About 30% of the above-ground surface has no Imagine texel, the blend ghosts by about a tenth where two drifted views meet, and the single-image mesh (IoU 0.445) is a looser fit than the four-view hull (IoU 0.543). A sprite of `yaw-090` shows that plate whole. The orbit’s gain is a camera that can move, with every drawn pixel still taken from a real view, and with magnification under 1.

Object classes:

- Hero objects the camera orbits or walks up to (this ship, a landmark): invisible mesh, Imagine views projected with `cos(yaw delta)` and zero past 45°. A region no allowed camera covers stays buried or off-camera.
- Tiny or far objects: a flat keyed card. A mesh there spends triangles to show one sprite.

## Biggest waste

The TripoSR load failed twice on the stack (unresolved config, then a transformers 5 attention rename) and once on a 5D triplane before the mesh existed. Those were setup misses, already fixed in `tools/mesh3d/triposr_run.py`. The shape that came out is the remaining limit. A second resolution was not run.

The viewer’s first two loads 404’d: `path.join` dropped the server root, then the root pointed at `tools/` instead of `tools/mesh3d`. `shot.mjs` serves `tools/mesh3d` now.
