# Imagine stills still needed

No Imagine access in this run. These faces already use an existing still where one fits. The ones below still read as flat grey and need their own native stills. Do not upscale. 1024×1024, four variants each, one tile = 0.5 m (2048 px/m).

## Lane shoulder slab

The wedges beside the lane from about 21 s to 42 s, and the grey triangles on the left near 24 s. Cool blue-grey glacier ice, matte, the colour of the wall ice already in the scene (not paper grey, not chrome). Fine cracked ice, no plates that repeat as columns. Top-down is wrong for these; they are tall faces, so the still should be a straight-on ice wall.

- Name: `shoulder-ice`
- Colour: muted blue-grey, near the existing glacier-ice stills
- Scale: 0.5 m across one 1024 px still
- Variants: 4
