# Corridor phone sprint — frame, continuity, speed, density, seam

One goal: a portrait sprint that stays framed like zone A, never teleports, and keeps getting faster and denser.

Done when:

1. Bolt’s chest on a 720×1600 portrait is within 0.03 screen-y of zone A’s chase (boom 6, eye 1.35, aim at 0.45 of Bolt’s height), centred, and above the joystick.
2. A 120 s sprint, straight and with turns, never jumps more than speed × dt × 1.5, and the live decor count never hits 0 or drops by more than 4 slots in one frame.
3. A held sprint reaches between 2× and 2.5× the 8.6 m/s top over 20–30 s. Births stay at or beyond 92 m.
4. Rocks per visible area at 24 s are greater than at 16 s, which are greater than at 8 s. A second instanced batch holds the extra copies. Draws stay at or under 12.
5. The far ground fogs into the horizon colour before the ground quad ends. Horizon hulls sit past that fog and break the line.
6. The plain play URL does not start a quest.
