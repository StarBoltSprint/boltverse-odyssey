# Living Archives / Echo Shards

One shared system for every zone and every player. Zone A (The Howling Eclipse) is the first manifest.

Done when:

1. A zone adds shards only by writing a manifest (id, position, Imagine image, title, English lore).
2. Bolt collects a shard by running near it. A card shows the Imagine shot and one lore line for 2–3 seconds. The run continues.
3. A paw button, off the joystick and off the camera-tilt swipe, opens Resume, Archives, Citadel (coming soon), and Settings.
4. The Archives screen is a reusable page over an Imagine painting of the Citadel Living Archives hall. Found shards are listed. Unfound shards are dim silhouettes.
5. Progress is per player in local storage, with a slot a later shared counter and Grok narration can fill without changing zones.
6. Zone A places 5 to 7 shards on the ground near the path, the monolith gate, the wreck hangar, and the old arch.
7. Manifest validation and save/load tests pass. Root npm test and playcheck pass.
8. Phone budgets stay honest: one extra instanced draw, no enlarged Imagine pixels, no camera shake, no invisible walls.
