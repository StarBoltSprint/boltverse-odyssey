# Zone B step 1g

Ember Mesa. Build the canyon layer by layer, then a photoreal planet.

1. Canyon walls and skyline. Tall sandstone walls and mesas frame the loop. A narrow slot, a basin rim, and a far mesa skyline. Seated on the relief. The rocks are the bounds.
2. Ground variety by relief. Cracked clay, sand ridges, rock shelves, wash gravel. Raised edges. No carpet. No visible repeat. Magnification at most 1.
3. Mid details. Hoodoos, fallen blocks, keyed pebbles and tufts near the path. The passages stay clear.
4. Sky life. The calm base stays. One subtle looping Imagine layer, thin cloud or dust.
5. Planet. One photoreal looping Imagine video. Golden-hour light, atmosphere, ring shadow, realistic moons. Bands turn. Rings stay. Keyed. Exactly one.
